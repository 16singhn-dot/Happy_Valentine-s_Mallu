Edit section videos (gift page)
===============================

The gift modals show one video per gift under "The Edit". The app looks for:

  edit1.mp4, edit2.mp4, edit3.mp4, edit4.mp4, edit5.mp4, edit6.mp4

in this folder (public/videos/).

To use your own videos:
  - Copy the video files from:  C:\Nirupam\Mallu\Edit
  - Paste them here and rename to edit1.mp4, edit2.mp4, ... edit6.mp4
    (first file → edit1.mp4, second → edit2.mp4, and so on).

Supported format: MP4 (H.264) works best in browsers.
